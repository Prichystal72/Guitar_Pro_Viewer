About

Song Bio

This song was originally written and performed by Bob Dylan for the soundtrack to the 1973 film Pat Garrett & Billy the Kid. GNR’s version is featured in 1990’s Days of Thunder.

The song is about an officer who accidentally shot a kid and is dying, maybe from suicide, and hoping he gets let into heaven, hence “Knockin’ on heaven’s door.” He knocks because he thinks he needs permission to enter here instead of Hell.

What did Guns N' Roses say about "Knockin’ on Heaven’s Door"?
Genius Answer

When I went to play the lead, Bob came up and asked me to play like… [incredulously] Django Reinhardt! l couldn’t figure out where he was coming from. I didn’t hear that at all! So basically, I just laid down the part I thought should be there. Everybody seemed to be happy with it. It was just a funny day, but the song got done.

—Slash

What movie played this song?
Genius Answer

Guns N' Roses recorded “Knockin' on Heaven’s Door” in 1990 for the soundtrack to the film Days of Thunder, starring Tom Cruise and Nicole Kidman. The song itself, however, was written nearly twenty years earlier by Bob Dylan for the soundtrack of the 1973 film Pat Garrett and Billy the Kid.
